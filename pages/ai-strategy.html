Operations Coordinator Agent
Monitors project status across tools, identifies bottlenecks, sends reminders, updates stakeholders, and generates weekly ops reports. Flags delays before they become problems.
Impact: Project delays cut by 30%. Real-time visibility without status meetings.
Data Analysis Agent
Connects to your databases, runs scheduled reports, identifies anomalies, generates insights in plain English, and alerts the team when KPIs deviate. No SQL required.
Impact: Decision time from days to hours. Proactive alerts instead of reactive reports.
Inventory & Fulfillment Agent
Tracks stock levels across locations, predicts reorder points, creates purchase orders, updates suppliers, and syncs with your e-commerce/ERP. Flags discrepancies automatically.
Impact: Stockouts reduced by 80%. Zero manual PO creation.
Onboarding Agent

Legal Review Agent

Impact: Contract turnaround from 5 days to 24 hours. Legal focuses on complex cases.
Marketing Campaign Agent
Builds campaigns from brief to launch: writes copy, generates images, sets up ads, defines audiences, and monitors performance. Pauses underperforming variants automatically.
Impact: Campaign launch time from 2 weeks to 2 days. ROAS improvement through auto-optimization.
