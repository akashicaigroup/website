What Is The AI Readiness Audit?

The AI Readiness Audit is a comprehensive operational diagnostic that evaluates your entire business across 10 critical dimensions. Over 5-7 days, we interview your team, analyze your systems, map your processes, and deliver a professional-grade report with clear scores, gap analysis, and a prioritized list of AI opportunities.

This is the smartest first step for any company serious about AI in 2026.

The 10-Dimension AI Readiness Assessment

01. Data Maturity

Where your data lives, its quality, structure, accessibility, and integration level across systems.

02. Process Documentation

How well your processes are documented versus tribal knowledge, including complexity and repetition level.

03. Tech Stack & Integrations

Current tools, API availability, system dependencies, and technical readiness for AI agents.

04. Team & Culture Readiness

Technical literacy, change readiness, workload capacity, and leadership buy-in.

05. Operational Bottlenecks

Where time is wasted, errors occur most frequently, and capacity limits growth.

06. Decision-Making Flows

Approval processes, decision speed, and where AI can safely act autonomously.

07. Customer Touchpoints

All interactions with customers and partners — response times, volume, and quality.

08. Compliance & Risk

Regulatory requirements, data privacy needs, and risk tolerance for AI implementation.

09. Budget & Resource Constraints

Current spend on manual labor, available AI budget, and realistic ROI expectations.

10. Growth & Scaling Readiness

Current growth rate, hiring plans, and operational limits preventing faster scaling.
