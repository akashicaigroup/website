Our Process

We don't guess. We diagnose.

From AI-curious to AI-native in four phases.

01

Diagnose

2-4 weeks

We audit your reality: operations, data infrastructure, team capabilities, and constraints. No assumptions.

  • Stakeholder interviews across departments
  • Data and systems architecture review
  • Process mapping and bottleneck identification
  • Competitive and market analysis
02

Blueprint

2-4 weeks

Your custom AI strategy. We identify the highest-leverage opportunities and architect the solution.

  • Opportunity prioritization matrix
  • Technical architecture design
  • Vendor/tool evaluation and selection
  • ROI modeling and risk assessment
  • Implementation roadmap with milestones
03

Build

3-6 months

We implement your pilot system. Custom development or strategic integration — whatever your reality requires.

  • Agile development sprints
  • Data pipeline construction
  • Model training or fine-tuning
  • Integration with existing systems
  • Testing and validation
04

Transfer

Ongoing

You own it. We train your team, document everything, and ensure you're independent. We stay available for optimization.

  • Team training and upskilling
  • Documentation and runbooks
  • Knowledge transfer sessions
  • Governance framework setup
  • Optional ongoing support retainer
